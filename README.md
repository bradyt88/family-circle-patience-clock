# Family Circle — Patience Clock

Standalone Family Circle branded Clock Patience card game.

## Core mechanics
- 52-card deck, randomly shuffled every game.
- 12 clock piles, four cards per pile.
- Four-card centre DRAW pile.
- Separate KINGS collection pile.
- Dealing is animated one card at a time clockwise from 1.
- Revealed cards remain above their original pile.
- A=1, 2–10=matching hour, J=11, Q=12.
- A King moves to KINGS; the next card comes from DRAW.
- The fourth King ends the game.
- No backend, accounts, adverts or dependency on the main Family Circle app.
